Insight gets lost between the field and the decision

MSLs capture real signal in the field every week — but by the time it reaches a brand or leadership team, most of it has been summarized, delayed, or dropped along the way. In conversations with MSL teams, prep for a single HCP interaction commonly runs 2–3 hours, with another 30–45 minutes afterward on documentation — time that isn't going toward the next interaction. And that time comes from somewhere: hours spent on prep and paperwork are hours not spent with an HCP, which means less field-facing time to capture the next insight in the first place.

More time with HCPs, better insight at the source

Every hour spent on prep and documentation is an hour not spent with an HCP. Credis isn't about producing more reporting — it's about giving MSLs back the scientific time that goes into thinner call prep and after-the-fact paperwork, so more of the week is spent where insight actually gets captured: in the room with the HCP.

This is exactly what the MSL Day Audit measures directly — the parallel free offer routed to the Head of MSL Excellence, not a deeper follow-on to the Leakage Map — how many hours a specific MSL's day could realistically go back to HCP-facing time.
